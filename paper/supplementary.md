# Supplementary: table of contents

All tables of the study are CSV files in `tables/`. This document lists every one of them, says what a row is, and says which part of the paper it supports, so that a reader can go from a statement in the Methods, Results or Discussion to the table behind it. The numbers in the main text are read from the tables listed here; each Results paragraph also names its source file in a hidden `src` comment in the Markdown.

## How the tables are organised

```
tables/
  INDEX.csv       one row per table: id, path, section, rows, licence flag, status
  MANIFEST.json   sha256 of every table, of the rules and of the generator
  audit/          A1-A5   which metric can be tested in which experiment, and why
  main/           T1-T9   the main-text tables (Tables 1 to 9 of the paper)
  supp/           S01-S25 supplementary tables, grouped by the subsection they support
  RULES.md, rules.json   the applicability rules (decided from inputs, definedness and design, never from results)
  spec/           small hand-curated inputs and derived aggregates the generator reads
```

Table ids follow the paper. `T1` to `T9` are the paper's Tables 1 to 9 (Methods Tables 1 and 2, then Results Tables 3 to 9: activity discrimination in zymogen pairs, activity discrimination in plated designs, substrate discrimination, activity ranking in BglB, activity ranking in the plated designs, detection ability on axis C, the chemistry ladder, and on axis E, second-shell removal); each is a complete table with provenance columns, and the paper prints a condensed version of some. `S` tables are grouped by the analysis they back, and their numbers were not changed when the Results were reordered: S17 to S19 back 3.1 (activity discrimination test; S17c and S17d are the complete versions of Tables 3 and 4), S14 to S14b back 3.2 (substrate discrimination test), S20 to S24 back 3.3 (activity ranking test), S04 to S13, S15 to S16 and S26 to S29 back 3.4 (detection ability test, by perturbation axis: structure-space metrics and the re-predicted ladder; S26 and S27 are Tables 8 and 9 with the supplementary comparators and the per-step and per-dose flags, S28 and S29 the former Tables 8 and 9), and S01 to S03, S08 and S25 cover metrics, tests and compute. The contents table below gives the Methods and Results subsection of every table. A letter after a number (S04a, S04b) marks tables of one analysis. `A` tables are the audit behind the main set of 33 metrics; `S01` and `S03` are reader-facing copies of `A1` and `A2`.

## Conventions shared by the tables

**Provenance columns.** Every result table carries the same block, so a row can be traced to its source and its denominator:
`test_id` (the test, translated in `S03`), `claim_type`, `source_artifacts` and `source_sha256` (the result files the row was read from, with the first 16 hex digits of their hash), `unit` and `n_units` (what was counted and how many), `n_clusters` and `cluster_def` (the resampling unit: enzyme sub-subclass for natural enzymes, campaign and plate well or backbone cluster for designs, position for BglB), `k`, `N` and `k_of_N` (a count with its denominator), `denominator_def`, `headline_ok` (1 if the row may be quoted as a headline result; 0 for per-metric detail, descriptive analyses, reference rows and wrong-ligand AUROCs), `status` and `licence_flag`.

**Claim types.** `detection` (can the metric see damage), `specificity` (catalytic against matched control), `equivalence` (panel ratio against 1), `discrimination` (a real contrast, dead against active), `ranking` (against a measured label), `enrichment`, `baseline` (a reference row), `design`, `audit`, `not_measurable`.

**Applicability status** (A3, S02, and the `status_a3` columns). `OK` can be tested; `OK-KNOCKON` tested but reported as displacement of the other catalytic residues, with no specificity verdict; `OK-PROVISIONAL` the raw status of the interface terms in the re-predicted ladder, resolved by testability (see `RULES.md`); `GLOBAL` tested but has no site input, so it is a comparator; `REF` a fixed reference row; `X-BOOKKEEP` a counter or constant; `X-NOINPUT` the input cannot reach the changed quantity; `X-ARM` the metric belongs to the other mode (structure space or prediction) than the test; `X-UNDEF-COV` defined on too few units; `X-WITHDRAWN` the test was withdrawn; `X-NOCTRL` no matched control exists; `X-CONFOUND` the control is confounded; `NOT-RUN`; `DUP` identical to another test and read once.

**Verdicts in the structure-space specificity tables.** `specific` (response significant, interval of the specificity ratio above 1, control adequate); `non_specific` (responds, not more than the control); `blind` (does not respond); `invariant` (does not change); and counts-floor labels for cells with too few units.

**Names that recur.** `canonical_key` is the distinct metric after literal aliases are merged (`metric_name` may be an alias); `in_main_set` is 1 for the 33 reported metrics; `level` or `rung` is the lesion step (isosteric 15.3 Å³, non-isosteric 36.4 Å³, Ala 53.2 Å³, Gly 80.5 Å³ median side-chain volume change; `second_shell_1/2/4` is the second-shell dose); `mode` `M1` means structure-space and `M3` prediction-based; `ame_flavour` is `crystal` (against the deposited structure) or `self_design` (against a design's own model) and the two are never pooled; `sr_*` is a specificity ratio with its interval; `auroc` is direction-free unless a column says `signed` or `directed`.

**Licence.** `licence_flag = D2DCure_aggregate` marks tables built from the D2DCure BglB data, whose licence is unstated: they hold metric-level aggregates only, no per-variant row (checked by `ops/verify_tables.py`). All other tables are `public`.

**Reproducibility.** `ops/make_tables.py` regenerates every table from committed result files, and `ops/verify_tables.py --determinism` re-derives key numbers independently and checks that a second run is byte-identical. The `source_artifacts` paths point into the private project (`results/...`) and do not resolve in a standalone copy of `tables/`.
